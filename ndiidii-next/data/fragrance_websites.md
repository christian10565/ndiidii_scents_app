## Search tips

- Use each store's search bar with the exact brand spelling, e.g. `Parfums de Marly`, `Narciso Rodriguez`, `Maison Francis Kurkdjian`, `Fragrance World`, or `Arabiyat Prestige`.
- For the **Oils** list, search both `perfume oil` and the fragrance name. Some stores list these under `concentrated oil`, `attar`, `roll-on`, or `inspired fragrance oil`.
- For the **Perfumes** list, search the exact brand and individual fragrance name; a store listing a brand does not guarantee every missing SKU is stocked.
